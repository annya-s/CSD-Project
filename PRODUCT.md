# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Farm operators who coordinate irrigation, labour, crop monitoring, resource allocation, and harvest planning are the primary users. Agronomists and crop specialists are secondary users who inspect evidence, investigate likely causes of stress, and validate recommendations.

## Product Purpose

FieldSignal is an early-warning and decision-support system for crop stress and yield risk. It combines field-level sensor readings, crop information, weather, historical crop performance, and multispectral imagery to show which farm areas need attention before stress is visually obvious. Success means users can identify the highest-risk area, understand why it matters, and act early without interpreting raw datasets.

## Positioning

Turn macro and micro crop signals into an early, explainable field-level action plan while keeping the farmer in control.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, and practical. The interface should feel like dependable agricultural operations software: technically capable, but written in plain language for decisions in the field.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic marketing page, a dense sensor console, unexplained AI scores, decorative farm imagery, or alarm-heavy visuals that make every anomaly feel equally urgent.

## Design Principles

- Lead with field condition and the next decision, not raw readings.
- Make risk spatial so users can see exactly where attention is needed.
- Explain every recommendation through evidence, confidence, assumptions, and limitations.
- Prioritise interventions by severity, likely yield impact, weather, resources, and growth stage.
- Keep the farmer in control through clear approval, rejection, and alternative-action paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use text and patterns in addition to colour for field status, preserve visible keyboard focus, support reduced motion, and keep the dashboard usable on desktop and mobile screens.
